# Child Safety Standards — Brawe Connect

**Developer:** Brawe Digital Hub  
**Application:** Brawe Connect (app.brawe.connect)  
**Last updated:** July 2026  
**Child Safety Contact:** safety@brawe.app

---

## Our Commitment

Brawe Connect is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward any content or behavior that exploits, harms, or endangers children.

---

## 1. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Brawe Connect **strictly and explicitly prohibits** all forms of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including but not limited to:

- Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) — any image, video, audio, text, or other content that sexually exploits minors;
- Grooming — attempting to befriend or establish trust with a minor for the purpose of sexual exploitation;
- Solicitation — requesting sexual content from or about minors, or arranging sexual encounters with minors;
- Trafficking — facilitating or promoting the trafficking of minors for sexual purposes;
- Non-consensual sexualization of minors — any content that sexualizes minors without consent or in a harmful manner.

Any user who violates this policy will be immediately and permanently banned from Brawe Connect, and the content will be reported to the appropriate law enforcement agencies, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and applicable local authorities.

---

## 2. Age Requirements

- Users must be at least **18 years old** to create an account on Brawe Connect.
- Brawe Connect is not intended for users under the age of 18.
- We reserve the right to terminate accounts we have reason to believe belong to minors.

---

## 3. User Reporting Mechanism

Brawe Connect provides users with in-app mechanisms to report content or behavior that violates our Child Safety Standards:

- **In-app reporting:** Every post, profile, and live stream has a "Report" button accessible to all users.
- **Direct contact:** Users can report concerns directly to our safety team at **safety@brawe.app**.
- Reports are reviewed by our Trust & Safety team within 24 hours. Content suspected of involving CSAM is immediately removed and reported to NCMEC via CyberTipline.

---

## 4. Handling of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Upon detection of CSAM — whether through user reports, automated systems, or proactive review:

1. The content is **immediately removed** from the platform;
2. The account responsible is **immediately suspended**;
3. A report is submitted to the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** through the CyberTipline (www.missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline);
4. All relevant data is preserved and provided to **law enforcement** upon request;
5. The incident is logged for internal audit and compliance review.

---

## 5. Compliance with Laws and Regulations

Brawe Connect complies with all applicable laws and regulations related to child safety, including:

- The **PROTECT Our Children Act** (United States);
- The **Brazilian Statute of the Child and Adolescent (ECA)** — Lei nº 8.069/1990;
- The **EU Directive on combating sexual abuse and exploitation of children** (2011/93/EU);
- Any other applicable national and international regulations.
