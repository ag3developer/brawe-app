Child Safety Standards

Developer: Brawe Digital Hub  |  App: Brawe Connect (app.brawe.connect)  |  Last updated: July 2026

Designated Child Safety Contact Point

This is the designated contact point for child safety issues at Brawe Digital Hub, including notifications from Google Play regarding CSAE content found in Brawe Connect. This representative is authorized to discuss our review and restriction procedures and to take immediate action when required.

safety@brawe.app

Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. CSAM reports are escalated immediately.

1. Our Commitment

Brawe Connect, developed by Brawe Digital Hub, is committed to providing a safe platform for all users. We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy toward any content, behavior, or activity that exploits, harms, or endangers children.

2. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Brawe Connect strictly and explicitly prohibits all forms of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) — any image, video, audio, text, or other content that sexually exploits minors;
  • Grooming — attempting to befriend or establish trust with a minor for the purpose of sexual exploitation;
  • Solicitation — requesting sexual content from or about minors, or arranging sexual encounters with minors;
  • Trafficking — facilitating or promoting the trafficking of minors for sexual purposes;
  • Non-consensual sexualization of minors — any content that sexualizes minors without consent or in a harmful manner.

Users who violate this policy will be immediately and permanently banned from Brawe Connect, and the content will be reported to the appropriate law enforcement agencies, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

3. Age Requirements

  • Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account on Brawe Connect.
  • Brawe Connect is not intended for users under the age of 18.
  • We reserve the right to terminate accounts we have reason to believe belong to minors.

4. In-App User Reporting Mechanism

Brawe Connect provides in-app tools to report harmful content or behavior:

  • Report button — available on every post, profile, and live stream;
  • Direct email — safety@brawe.app for urgent child safety reports;
  • Reports are reviewed by our Trust & Safety team within 24 hours.

5. Handling of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Upon detection of CSAM — through reports, automated systems, or proactive review:

  1. Content is immediately removed from the platform;
  2. The responsible account is immediately suspended;
  3. A report is filed with NCMEC via CyberTipline;
  4. All data is preserved and provided to law enforcement upon request;
  5. The incident is logged for internal audit and compliance review.

6. Legal Compliance

Brawe Connect complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including:

  • The Brazilian Statute of the Child and Adolescent (ECA) — Lei nº 8.069/1990;
  • The PROTECT Our Children Act (United States);
  • The EU Directive 2011/93/EU on combating sexual abuse and exploitation of children;
  • Any other applicable national and international regulations.

7. Policy Enforcement

Violations of these Child Safety Standards will result in:

  • Immediate content removal;
  • Permanent account termination;
  • Reporting to NCMEC and law enforcement when required by law;
  • Legal action where applicable.
